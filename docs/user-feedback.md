# First usability sessions

This is a plan and a blank template. No sessions have been conducted and no user feedback is claimed.

## Invite two engineering managers

Use this invitation yourself, or adapt it for someone you know:

> I built a small Jira sprint dashboard and would value 15 minutes of candid feedback. Could you try it with fictional data and tell me what you would act on, what is unclear, and whether importing a CSV would fit your workflow? You do not need to share company data.

Use the [local setup](../setup.md), the fictional demo, and [synthetic CSV](../examples/sprint.csv). The hosted copy is private; do not assume a participant can open it. Share your screen or run the app locally. Ask before recording a session and avoid collecting ticket content, names, or company-confidential details.

## Watch before explaining

Give the participant one task at a time. Avoid explaining the intended meaning before they answer.

1. Look at the fictional sprint. What would you ask the team about first, and why?
2. Find a ticket that needs follow-up. What would you do next?
3. Import the synthetic CSV and correct Discovery to In Progress.
4. Explain what Unavailable means for original commitment and scope changes. What do the estimated dates tell you?
5. Describe where this would fit in your sprint review, and what would stop you using it.

Record what happened separately from your interpretation. A suggestion from one participant is a lead to investigate, not proof of demand.

## Decide what to change

After the sessions, compare recurring misunderstandings and workflow barriers. Choose one concrete improvement, explain the evidence behind it, and define how you will check whether it helped. Keep the sample size and uncertainty visible. Do not add adoption, time-saved, or delivery-improvement claims without measuring them.
